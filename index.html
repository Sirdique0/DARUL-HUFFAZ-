OUR RULES & REGULATIONS

  • Fear Allah in all your actions.
  • Be punctual and attend all classes regularly.
  • Respect your teachers, parents, and fellow students.
  • Wear the prescribed school uniform neatly and modestly.
  • Maintain personal hygiene and keep the school environment clean.
  • Speak politely and avoid fighting or abusive language.
  • Take good care of all school property and facilities.
  • Perform your daily prayers and participate actively in Qur'an lessons.
  • Complete all assignments and come prepared for every class.
  • Be honest, trustworthy, and avoid all forms of cheating.
